`master/saas-sellability-master-checklist.pdf` shows all five stages side by side on one page. Zoom into your column.

## How the score works

Every item is worth 1 point; items buyers weight most are worth 2. Add the points for the boxes you tick. Each edition shows the three bands for its stage. At the first three stages, two tests override the score: annual gross revenue retention under 70%, or a 2 to 3 year ARR CAGR under 20% with any flat or down year.
