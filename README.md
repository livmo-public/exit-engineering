# Exit Engineering

Free resources from LIVMO for founders who want to engineer an exit: how to prepare from the start, what to build in the years before a sale, what to fix 12 months before going to market, how to read your current valuation, and how the process itself runs.

## Contents

- `saas/saas-sellability-checklist/`: the SaaS Sellability Checklist. Five detailed editions, one per ARR range, and one master checklist with all five stages side by side.
