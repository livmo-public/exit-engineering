## FAQ

### How do I prepare my SaaS company for sale?

Read your SaaS the way a buyer reads it. The [SaaS Sellability Checklist](saas/saas-sellability-checklist/) lists what buyers check at each ARR stage. Tick what is true today, add your points, and fix the gaps that the next stage asks for.

### What do buyers look for in a SaaS business at each ARR stage?

The checklist groups buyer checks into 8 areas: revenue quality, customers, unit economics, financial quality, product and technology, owner and team, legal and IP, and market. In the checklist, two tests override the score at the first three stages: annual gross revenue retention under 70%, or a 2 to 3 year ARR compound annual growth rate (CAGR) under 20% with any flat or down year.

### What is a Quality of Earnings report?

A Quality of Earnings (QoE) report tests whether the earnings in your financial statements are real and repeatable. It adjusts EBITDA for owner and one-time costs, ties revenue to cash in the bank, and checks working capital. The [QoE Readiness](qoe-readiness/) skill helps you prepare your files and find problems before a buyer or lender does.

### When does an SBA lender require a QoE report?

Under SBA SOP 50 10 8.1, effective October 1, 2026, an SBA lender must get an independent QoE report when a new owner buys a business for $3M or more. The report is for the lender. The seller cannot prepare it or have it prepared. The [Buyer Debt Capacity Calculator](buyer-debt-capacity/) lists the source and the date checked.

### Do I need deferred revenue on my books?

Yes, if customers prepay for annual or multi-year SaaS contracts. Cash arrives when you bill, but you earn the revenue month by month. Without a deferred revenue account, revenue looks too high in billing months, and a QoE adjusts it. The QoE Readiness skill flags annual billing with no deferred revenue.

### How much debt can a buyer use to buy my business?

A lender sizes the loan on cash flow, not on revenue or ARR multiples. The [Buyer Debt Capacity Calculator](buyer-debt-capacity/) starts from profit and adds back owner and one-time costs. It subtracts an operator salary and capital spending. It then divides the cash by the lender's coverage target. For an SBA loan to a new owner, the coverage target is at least 1.25.

### What are add-backs, and why does owner pay matter?

Add-backs are costs in your P&L that a new owner would not pay, such as personal expenses or one-time legal costs. A buyer still pays someone to run the business. So the calculator adds back owner pay only when an operator salary replaces it.

### What is my SaaS company worth?

The [LIVMO SaaS Valuation Calculator](https://saasvaluation.livmo.com) estimates a value from comparable deal multiples. It gives a range of plus or minus 35% and a 0 to 100 exit readiness score.
